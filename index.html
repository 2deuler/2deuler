Existence and nonuniqueness

Two-dimensional Euler equations

∂tu+div(u⊗u)+∇p =0, divu =0.

These equations describe the motion of an ideal fluid with velocity u(t, x) and pressure p(t, x), for x ∈ ℝ2.

Here we collect AI-assisted manuscripts and their source material on the existence and nonuniqueness of weak solutions.
