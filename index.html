Existence and nonuniqueness

Two-dimensional Euler equations

∂tu+div(u⊗u)+∇p =0, divu =0.

These equations describe the motion of an ideal fluid with velocity u(t, x) and pressure p(t, x), for x ∈ ℝ2 and t > 0, with initial condition u(0, x) = u0(x). Here we collect AI-assisted manuscripts and their source material on the existence and nonuniqueness of weak solutions.

01

Delort’s question: sign-changing vortex-sheet data

A manuscript proposing global weak Euler solutions for arbitrary finite signed initial vorticity in H−1, with a strong initial velocity trace and integrable vorticity at positive times.

02

Non-unique vanishing-viscosity solutions of 2D Euler with vorticity in Lp

A manuscript proposing two distinct Euler solutions obtained as vanishing-viscosity limits from the same initial vorticity in L1 ∩ Lp, for each 2 < p < ∞.
