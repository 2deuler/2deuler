Let $\mathcal M(\mathbb R^2)$ denote the space of finite signed\

\\[\
&#x20;   K(x)=\frac{(-x_2,x_1)}{2\pi|x|^2}.\
\\]\
For every\
\\[\
&#x20;   \omega_0\in\mathcal M(\mathbb R^2)\cap H^{-1}(\mathbb R^2),\
&#x20;   \qquad u_0=K\*\omega_0,\
\\]\
with no sign restriction on $\omega_0$, does there exist a global\
weak solution of the incompressible Euler equations\
\\[\
&#x20;   u\in C_w([0,\infty);\
&#x20;            L^2\_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R^2))\
\\]\
with $u(0)=u_0$ and $\operatorname{div}u=0$, whose vorticity\
$\omega(t):=\partial_1u_2(t)-\partial_2u_1(t)$ satisfies\
\\[\
\begin{gathered}\
&#x20;   \omega(t)\in\mathcal M(\mathbb R^2),\
&#x20;   \qquad u(t)=K\*\omega(t)\
&#x20;   \qquad\text{for every }t\geq0,\\\\\
&#x20;   \sup\_{0\leq t\leq T}\\|\omega(t)\\|\_{\mathcal M}<\infty\
&#x20;   \qquad\text{for every }T>0?\
\end{gathered}\
\\]\
Here the Euler equations are required in the sense that\

&#x20;   \int_0^T\\!\\!\int\_{\mathbb R^2}\

&#x20;   +\int\_{\mathbb R^2}u_0\cdot\varphi(0,\cdot)\\,dx=0\
\\]\
