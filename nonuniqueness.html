The manuscript’s main claim

Two distinct vanishing-viscosity limits

This manuscript contains a claim of the following result:

Theorem

For every p ∈ (2, ∞), there exists a compactly supported vorticity ωin∈Lp(ℝ2) and a sequence of viscosities {νn}n ∈ ℕ with νn ↓ 0 as n → ∞, such that the associated solutions of the 2D Navier-Stokes equations in vorticity form

{∂tωνn+uνn·∇ωνn=νnΔωνn,ωνn|t=0=ωin,

satisfy

ων2n→ωe,
ων2n+1→ωo,
ωe≠ωo.

The convergences hold in the space C([0,T];Lp(ℝ2)) for some T ∈ (0, ∞), and ωe, ωo are two distinct weak solutions of the 2D Euler equations with initial vorticity ωin.

Our purpose

We are sharing the manuscript here because we would like the mathematics behind this result to be prioritized over a counterproductive race to upload AI-generated proofs to arXiv.

We are organizing a reading group to study the claimed proof in detail, identify and deeply understand its main underlying ideas, and communicate them in a form more accessible to the mathematical community, for instance as a set of lecture notes.
